OUR STORY

Built to bring players together

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena began with Jason Naidoo's idea: create a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages can enjoy current technology, compete in esports and spend time with friends.

The arena welcomes casual players, competitive gamers, schools and corporate groups. Its packages and experiences make it easier to find a session that fits the occasion.

OUR VISION

Gaming experiences for every player

A welcoming arena where people can discover, practise and share the excitement of gaming.

OUR MISSION

Make play accessible

Offer engaging experiences with clear information, quality equipment and helpful support.

OUR GOALS

Play. Compete. Connect.

Support casual play, esports development, school activities and team events.
